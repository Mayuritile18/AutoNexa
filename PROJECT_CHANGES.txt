AutoNexa - Final Frontend Requirement Changes

Design/CSS: Existing style.css and admin-style.css are preserved. No redesign.
JavaScript: Not added.

Final concept:
- Admin: one administrator manages users/staff and all records.
- Users: multiple garage staff/operators enter and update operational data.
- Customer -> Vehicles -> Services.
- Services record staff, work done, cost, payment method/status and service status.
- Next service and reminders are linked to vehicle.
- Service history is shown from service records; no separate service_history table is required.

Final database tables planned for backend:
1 users
2 staff
3 customers
4 vehicles
5 services
6 service_work
7 next_services
8 reminders
9 payments
